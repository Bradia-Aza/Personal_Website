My research interests are centered around how AI systems learn useful representations of the world. With a background in computer vision, I’m particularly interested in world models, representation learning, and multimodal systems, and in how we can move beyond treating different forms of information as simply collections of tokens. I’m interested in understanding how models can build more structured and efficient representations, learn from different modalities, and develop a richer understanding of the environments they interact with.
